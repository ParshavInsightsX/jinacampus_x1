# A. Draft Public Privacy Notice

## 1. Who we are

JinaCampus is a school-management software service operated by `[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]`. Schools and educational institutions use JinaCampus to manage authorised school operations. Depending on the processing activity and applicable law, the institution and JinaCampus may have different data-protection roles. Those roles must be confirmed in the institution agreement and DPA.

## 2. Personal data processed

Depending on enabled modules and the user's relationship with an institution, JinaCampus may process account and contact details; institution/branch and role information; student and guardian records; enrollment, attendance and academic information; staff records; uploaded school documents; communications and preferences; authentication/security information such as sessions, IP address and user agent; audit logs; and, when approved modules are enabled, billing/transaction records.

JinaCampus is designed to minimise sensitive identifiers. Full Aadhaar numbers, full bank-account details and payment-card credentials must not be collected or stored unless a separately approved lawful and secure design is implemented. Existing student-record workflows retain masked references rather than plaintext full values.

## 3. Why data is processed

Purposes may include providing the institution's contracted school-management service; authenticating authorised users; maintaining student, guardian, academic and staff records; attendance; communications selected by the institution/user; security, fraud prevention and auditability; support; legal compliance; service administration; and billing where enabled.

The final notice must map each purpose to the applicable legal ground and clearly distinguish processing instructed/required by an institution from optional processing that requires consent.

## 4. Children and students

JinaCampus applies heightened safeguards to student data. Student information is not sold and must not be used for targeted advertising to children. Access is restricted to authorised institutional users and linked guardians/students where such portals are enabled. JinaCampus will follow the institution's lawful instructions and applicable child-data requirements. Any reliance on an educational-institution exemption or parental/guardian consent mechanism requires written legal approval for the specific processing purpose.

## 5. Sharing and subprocessors

Data may be shared with contracted service providers only as necessary to operate JinaCampus, such as hosting/database/storage, communications, security/monitoring, support and payment providers when enabled. The approved public subprocessor list must identify actual providers and relevant processing locations. Vendors must be contractually restricted from independent use of school/student data except where legally required.

## 6. Retention

Personal data is retained only for approved operational, contractual and legal purposes. The final retention periods are governed by the JinaCampus Retention Schedule, institution instructions, legal holds and applicable law. JinaCampus must not publish a generic retention promise that conflicts with school, employment, tax, security or other recordkeeping duties.

## 7. Security

JinaCampus uses administrative, technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including tenant-scoped access controls, role/permission checks, authentication controls, audit logging and infrastructure security. No statement should claim that security is absolute or that a certification exists unless it has been independently obtained and remains valid.

## 8. Rights and requests

Subject to applicable law and the institution/JinaCampus role for the data, an eligible person may request access, correction/completion/update, erasure, grievance redressal, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, and other rights available under applicable law. Requests involving a student/guardian relationship require authority verification and tenant-safe routing. Contact `[PRIVACY_CONTACT]` or use `[PRIVACY_REQUEST_URL]` once implemented.

## 9. Complaints and grievance contact

Privacy questions and grievances may be sent to `[GRIEVANCE_CONTACT]`. The final notice must state the required contact details and escalation route under applicable law.

## 10. International processing

The final notice must disclose material cross-border processing based on the actual infrastructure/subprocessor inventory and any applicable government restrictions. Do not assume all vendors process data only in India.

## 11. Changes

Material notice changes must receive a new version/effective date and be communicated as legally/contractually required. Acceptance or consent must not be silently inferred where fresh consent is legally required.

# B. Draft SaaS Terms of Service

## 1. Contract and authorised users

These Terms govern use of JinaCampus by an institution and its authorised users together with the applicable Order Form, DPA, SLA/support terms and incorporated policies. If documents conflict, the final contract must state an approved order of precedence.

The institution controls who receives school-user access and must promptly remove access that is no longer authorised. Users must protect credentials, must not share accounts improperly and must comply with role/permission restrictions.

## 2. Service

JinaCampus provides the modules/features stated in the applicable Order Form. Features described as beta, pilot, future, disabled or not purchased are not committed production services. The institution remains responsible for its educational/administrative decisions and the accuracy/lawfulness of data and instructions it provides, subject to JinaCampus's own statutory and contractual responsibilities.

## 3. Fees, taxes and renewal

Fees, taxes, invoicing, payment dates, trial terms, renewal, price changes, suspension for non-payment, cancellation and refunds must be stated in the Order Form and approved commercial policy. Do not activate auto-renewal or online checkout until disclosures and consent/acceptance evidence have been legally reviewed.

## 4. Acceptable use

Users may not attempt unauthorised access; bypass tenant/RBAC restrictions; probe or disrupt the service except under an authorised security-testing programme; upload unlawful or malicious material; misuse student/staff information; impersonate another user; scrape data without authorisation; share credentials in violation of policy; or use JinaCampus to violate applicable law or institutional safeguarding duties.

## 5. Data and privacy

Each party must comply with the DPA and applicable data-protection law. Institution data remains subject to the ownership/control terms approved in the agreement. JinaCampus receives only the licence/rights necessary to provide, secure, support and lawfully improve the contracted service as specifically approved; no broad right to commercialise student data is granted.

## 6. Intellectual property

JinaCampus software, branding, documentation and platform technology remain owned/licensed by `[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]` and its licensors. The institution retains its rights in institution/customer content. Feedback rights, custom-development ownership, third-party components and trademark use require final approved language.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other's confidential information with reasonable safeguards and use it only for the contract. Statutory disclosures should be limited and notified where legally permitted.

## 8. Security and incidents

JinaCampus will maintain the security measures stated in the DPA/security schedule and will cooperate on qualifying incidents as required by law and contract. The final contract must not promise a notification period that prevents compliance with shorter statutory deadlines.

## 9. Availability, backups and support

Service levels, maintenance, support hours, backup frequency, RPO/RTO, service credits and exclusions are only those in the approved SLA. Marketing or sales materials must not claim a higher availability or recovery commitment.

## 10. Suspension and termination

JinaCampus may suspend access only on grounds approved in the final contract, such as material security risk, unlawful use or qualifying non-payment, with notice/cure where appropriate. On termination, export/return/deletion follows the DPA, retention schedule, legal holds and agreed offboarding period.

## 11. Warranties, liability, indemnity and disputes

**COUNSEL / AUTHORISED SIGNATORY DECISION REQUIRED.** Insert negotiated warranty disclaimers, liability caps/exclusions, indemnities, governing law, jurisdiction/arbitration, force majeure and notices. These provisions must not be generated or accepted automatically by engineering.

# D. Draft Acceptable Use Policy

Users must use JinaCampus only for authorised institutional purposes. Prohibited conduct includes unauthorised access to another tenant, branch, user or student; credential sharing that defeats accountability; malicious code; attempts to disable security/audit controls; unauthorised bulk export or disclosure; unlawful harassment/content; misuse of communications; collection of data not needed for school operations; and attempts to use JinaCampus for surveillance or profiling outside an approved lawful institutional purpose.

Security researchers must use an approved vulnerability-disclosure channel once published and must not access, retain, alter or disclose real student/staff data.

# E. Draft Cookie and Tracking Notice

JinaCampus currently requires essential browser/app storage for authentication, security and core functionality. Before publication, Product/Privacy must complete an actual cookie/local-storage/SDK inventory for web, PWA and mobile.

Categories must be documented as:

1. **Strictly necessary / security** — session/authentication, CSRF/security, tenant context where technically necessary.
2. **Preferences** — only where actually implemented.
3. **Analytics** — disabled until provider, purpose, data fields, retention, location and consent/legal basis are approved.
4. **Advertising/marketing** — must not be enabled for child/student experiences; any public-site marketing technology requires separate review.

The notice must state actual cookie/storage names, provider, purpose and duration. Do not publish generic cookie claims copied from another product.

# G. Draft Child / Student Data Notice

JinaCampus helps a student's school manage authorised school activities such as enrollment records, attendance, academic administration and communications. The school decides which authorised staff can use the system for school purposes. JinaCampus restricts access using accounts and permissions and keeps records of important changes.

JinaCampus does not sell student personal information or use a student's school account for targeted advertising. Students and guardians should not share passwords. A guardian who needs to ask about a student's information should use the school's approved process or `[PRIVACY_REQUEST_URL]` when available. JinaCampus may need to verify that the requester is authorised to act for the student.

The final student-facing version must be tested for age-appropriate readability and translated/localised where the institution requires it.

# H. Draft Grievance / Privacy Request Procedure

1. Intake request and timestamp it.
2. Classify privacy, security, billing, service or institutional-record issue.
3. Resolve tenant/institution without trusting user-supplied tenant identifiers alone.
4. Verify identity and, for student requests, guardian/authorised relationship.
5. Determine whether JinaCampus can act directly or must coordinate with the institution.
6. Check legal hold, statutory retention and third-party processor impact.
7. Execute approved access/correction/export/erasure/consent action with least privilege.
8. Record decision, reason, approver and audit evidence.
9. Respond within the legally/contractually applicable period.
10. Preserve escalation rights and close only after evidence is complete.

Privacy request data itself is confidential and tenant-scoped.
