# Institution Service Agreement and Order Schedule

Status: **Draft for legal and commercial approval**
Version: `draft-0.1`

This document lists the minimum schedules required for a JinaCampus institution contract. It deliberately does not invent prices, liability limits, governing law, service levels, or refund promises.

## Contract Schedules

### A. Parties and Order

- Institution legal name, registration, address, tax details, authorised signatory.
- JinaCampus operating legal entity, registration, address, tax details, authorised signatory.
- Subscription start/end, pilot/trial status, enabled institutions and branches.
- Enabled modules, feature entitlements, user/storage/usage limits, add-ons.
- Implementation, migration, training, support and acceptance scope.

### B. Fees and Subscription Lifecycle

- Currency, price, taxes, invoice and due dates.
- Trial, renewal, upgrade, downgrade, grace, suspension and cancellation rules.
- Refund policy and non-refundable items, subject to applicable law.
- Usage overages, provider pass-through costs and billing safeguards.
- No provider-dependent feature is billable or represented as live until configured and accepted.

### C. Service Levels and Support

- Support hours, channels, severity definitions and response targets.
- Planned maintenance and emergency security maintenance.
- Availability measurement, exclusions and any approved service credits.
- Backup scope, recovery mechanism, RPO, RTO and customer responsibilities.
- Escalation contacts, incident communications and status reporting.

### D. Institution Responsibilities

- Accurate records and lawful collection.
- Parent/guardian and staff notices, authority and consent.
- Least-privilege role assignment and timely access revocation.
- Device, network, export, password/passkey and acceptable-use controls.
- State, board, employment, finance, tax and record-retention obligations.
- Review of generated reports and academic/attendance decisions before reliance.

### E. JinaCampus Responsibilities

- Operate the contracted modules with tenant isolation and server-side permissions.
- Maintain documented security, audit, incident, change and support procedures.
- Process data under the DPA and maintain an approved subprocessor register.
- Preserve disabled-module data according to the retention and exit schedules.
- Avoid representing deferred modules/providers as operational.

### F. Data, Export, Exit, and Deletion

- Data ownership and limited service licence.
- Standard export formats, timing and authorised delivery.
- Transition assistance, archive window, legal hold and backup expiry.
- Verified deletion instruction and completion evidence.
- Treatment of audit, security, billing and legal records after exit.

### G. Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

- JinaCampus software/brand ownership and institution-content ownership.
- Feedback and configuration rights.
- Open-source and third-party components.
- Confidential information, permitted disclosure and survival.

### H. Legal Terms Requiring Signatory Approval

- Representations and warranties.
- Disclaimers and limitation of liability.
- Indemnities and insurance.
- Force majeure.
- Governing law, jurisdiction, dispute resolution and notices.
- Assignment, subcontracting, changes, precedence, severability and waiver.

## Pre-Signature Gate

- [ ] Legal entity and authority verified.
- [ ] Order scope and entitlements match the application configuration.
- [ ] DPA and subprocessor register attached.
- [ ] Retention, security, incident and recovery schedules approved.
- [ ] Prices, taxes, renewal, cancellation and refunds approved.
- [ ] Pilot/production limitations disclosed.
- [ ] No unsupported SLA or board-compliance claim included.
- [ ] Both signatories and evidence references recorded.
