# SchoolCast Deferred Capability Backlog

## Purpose

This is the product and technical backlog for SchoolCast capabilities excluded from the no-premium release scope. Every item remains disabled server-side and must not be presented as operational until its activation conditions are met.

| Deferred capability | Premium or unavailable dependency | Reason for exclusion | User/workflow impact | Current fallback | Future approach | Activation conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live WhatsApp delivery | Approved WhatsApp Business provider, conversation billing, verified business/number, templates, webhook operations | Sender, consent, template, billing, legal/privacy, and recipient-pilot evidence is absent | No WhatsApp notices, attendance alerts, homework, or reports | Authenticated in-app notices only | Meta Cloud API or approved BSP with server-only secrets and signed webhooks | Approved sender/templates/consent/billing; rate-limit, webhook, opt-out, monitoring, and real-recipient pilot pass |
| Live email delivery | Verified sender/domain, email provider account, billing/quotas, webhook operations | Sender ownership, complaint/bounce handling, billing, and pilot evidence is absent | No external email notices or reports | Authenticated in-app notices only | Approved provider such as Resend or a maintained SMTP service | Domain verification, consent, templates, webhook signature/replay, quota, suppression, monitoring, and pilot pass |
| Scheduled publication | Two reliable worker/scheduler failure domains and external monitoring | No approved production worker topology or on-call operation exists | Messages must be published immediately by an authorised user | Immediate governed publication | Two independent self-hosted workers with PostgreSQL leases, external dead-man checks, alerts, and DLQ operations | Capacity, failover, restart, pool, alert, DLQ, backup, and on-call drills pass |

## Reconsideration Rule

No backlog item may be enabled because its code exists. Activation requires evidence for its dependency, server-side release-scope approval, tenant-level feature approval, RBAC regression testing, rollback verification, and named operational ownership.
